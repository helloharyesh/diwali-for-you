Shubh Deepawali

Happy Diwali

to the light of my life,

I made this little world of lights, flowers and fireworks just for you.

Before the lamps are lit…

Every diya tonight pushes back a little darkness. But long before today, you were the one who made my ordinary days glow.

You are the calm in my loudest moments, the reason I smile at my phone, and the warmest light I have ever known.

So this Diwali, I don’t need a thousand lamps. I just need you beside me.

A small film, made with code

Turn your screen up, it’s 24 seconds

Three wishes for you

May your days be bright

Like sunflowers, may you always find the sun and turn your face toward it, even on cloudy days.

May your heart stay soft

Like a violet lily at dusk, calm, graceful and quietly beautiful. Never change that.

May every dream find you

Every big idea, every late-night hope, every wish you whisper to the sky tonight.

Why you are my Diwali

You bring light

Wherever you walk in, the room feels warmer.

You bring colour

My life was a sketch. You filled it in with violet, gold and laughter.

You bring home

Wherever you are is where I want to be, today and every festival after.

Happy Diwali, my love

Thank you for being my light, my peace and my favourite person. May this festival fill your life with love, success and endless joy.

I promise to keep lighting a little lamp for you, every single day.

Forever yours, ❤
